## 2. Global Architecture & Oral Defense Highlights

Key architectural strengths to present to the panel:
1. **Multi-Role Security Perimeter:** Enforced database Row Level Security (RLS) and UI `RoleGate` protecting Admin, Dean, Registrar, Faculty, and Student workspaces.
2. **Dual-Key Grade Release Mechanism:** Faculty computes grades with automated transmutation; grades are submitted with an irreversible anti-tamper lock and released only after Registrar clearance.
3. **Automated Academic Progression Engine:** Database-level function `fn_evaluate_student_year_level` scans completed curriculum courses and automatically promotes passing students from Year 1 to Year 2 upon school year rollover.
4. **Context-Aware AI Assistant Drawer:** Conversational AI dynamically grounded in the active user's role, permissions, and enrolled courses.
5. **Universal Multi-Step Wizards & Bulk CSV Pipelines:** Built-in wizard modal engines and CSV batch importers across 10 modules.
6. **Mobile-First Responsive Layout:** Full parity across Desktop (`1440x900`) and Mobile (`390x844`) with dedicated touch drawers and zero horizontal overflow.
