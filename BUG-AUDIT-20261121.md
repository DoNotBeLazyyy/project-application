## Tier 1: wrong grades, or a feature that is broken

### Grades and GWA
1. ✅ **Special grades count as 0 in GWA.** INC/DRP periods are stored with final_grade 0, and `fn_student_course_grades` averages them in. On a 1.00–5.00 scale, 0 looks better than 1.00, so INC can raise the Insight GWA and qualify a student for honors. The transcript excludes them, so the two pages disagree.
2. ✅ **Roster GWA and Dean's List badge ignore period weights** (`fn_compute_student_gwa`). Each released period adds the full course units, so Prelim, Midterm and Finals count equally, and raw % is used when there is no transmuted grade. This can award the Dean's List badge to a student who isn't eligible.
3. ◻️ **A partly released course counts as final** (`fn_student_course_grades`). Releasing only Prelim makes it the course grade for GWA, transcript, audit ("Completed"), failing count, honors "Blocked", and the year-level sync (a student can be promoted mid-term).
4. ✅ **Students see Approved grades before release** (`fn_list_my_grades`, `fn_get_subject_grades`, `utils/grade-display.util.ts:3`). My Grades shows them with a Released badge, but the breakdown then says "not released yet". The dashboard count leaves them out.
5. ✅ **Evaluation releases only one period** (`fn_release_grades_after_evaluation`). With term-scope evaluation, Midterm and Finals stay Approved forever after the student evaluates. They are missing from the transcript, the enrollment is never finalized, and no notification is sent.
6. ◻️ **Rubric scores aren't scaled** (`fn_grade_submission_rubric`). 20/20 on a 20-pt rubric for a 100-pt essay counts as 20%.
7. ◻️ **Grade scale on screen differs from the database** (`student/grade/use-transmutation-scale.ts`). Special rows become "0.00" steps, all school years are mixed, and steps are matched differently. The What-If simulator offers "0.00 INC", and a 97.5% result page shows no grade.
8. ◻️ **Honor targets come from any school year** (`fn_get_student_insight`). Another year's Cum Laude threshold can be used.
9. ◻️ **Live breakdown differs from the released grade** (`fn_get_my_grade_breakdown`). It recomputes from current scores and `now()`, so after a later regrade or a missed close date the components no longer add up to the released grade.

## Policy decisions (decided 2026-11-21, following usual Philippine university practice)

Where screens disagree, every screen will follow these rules:

1. **Special grades (INC, DRP, W, NFE) never count in GWA, honors or Dean's List.** They aren't numbers. The transcript already works this way; Insight, the roster and the What-If simulator will match it.
2. **Students see a grade only once it is Released.** Registrar release is the official act; Approved grades stay hidden on every student screen.
3. **A course grade is final only when every period of its term is Released.** Until then, screens say "In progress". Failing, honors, earned units and year level use final course grades only.
4. **GWA everywhere means:** period-weighted course grades, then units-weighted across courses, with special grades left out. The roster, Dean's List, Insight and transcript all use this one formula.
5. **Attendance rate = (sessions − absences) ÷ sessions.** Excused counts as attended and Late counts as present. One formula on every screen.
6. **A rubric score is scaled to the assessment's Total Points.** 18/20 on a rubric for a 100-point essay = 90 points.
7. **Once a period's grades are Submitted, faculty can't change scores in that period.** The registrar uses "Return to Faculty" to reopen them, which makes the released grade and its breakdown always agree.
8. **Grade encoding dates are enforced by the database for faculty**, not only hidden in the UI. Registrar and Admin can still act outside the window.
9. **Registrar review is mandatory.** A scheduled release only releases grades the registrar has Approved. It never auto-approves grades submitted later. If a release date is set, Approve & Release waits for it.
10. **An ungraded component counts as 0%, with warnings.** Unchanged from 2026-11-20.

## Your decision (not clearly bugs). Superseded by the policy decisions above

- **Scheduled grade release keeps running.** The `grade-release-sweep` job runs every 15 minutes in the live database. After a period's release date, any grade a faculty member submits is released to students with no registrar review. This is useful for grades held back by pending evaluations, but it also skips review of late submissions.
- **Approve & Release ignores the scheduled release time** and reveals grades immediately.
- **Grade encoding window is enforced only in the UI**; the database accepts calculate and submit after the window closes.
