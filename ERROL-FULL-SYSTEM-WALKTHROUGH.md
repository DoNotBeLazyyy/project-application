## 3. Global Architecture & Oral Defense Highlights

Present these key architectural strengths:
1. **Multi-Role Security Perimeter:** Strict role-based workspace protection separating Admin, Dean, Registrar, Faculty, and Student interfaces.
2. **Operational Calendar Continuity:** Uses the existing active academic year `AY-2027-2028` (1st Semester Ongoing) across all live modules.
3. **Dual-Key Grade Release Mechanism:** Faculty computes grades with automated transmutation; grades are submitted with an irreversible anti-tamper lock and released only after Registrar clearance.
4. **Context-Aware AI Assistant Drawer:** Grounded dynamically in user permissions, active role, and enrolled sections.
5. **Universal Multi-Step Wizards & Bulk CSV Pipelines:** Built-in guided step-by-step wizards and CSV batch importers across 10 modules.
6. **Zero Namespace Collisions:** Prefixed strictly with `ERROL-` so your data never interferes with other presenters or test accounts.
